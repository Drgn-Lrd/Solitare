How To Play

Mahjong Solitaire

Clear the whole board by matching tiles in pairs. The built-in boards use a full 144-tile set; some custom layouts (Change Layout) may use fewer.

  • Tap a tile to select it, then tap a matching tile to remove both.
  • Only free tiles can be selected — nothing covering it from above, and at least one open side (left or right).
  • Two tiles match if they're the exact same tile, or:
    both are Flowers — any Flower matches any Flower.
    or
    both are Seasons — same idea.
  • Hint pulses any one currently-available matching pair.
  • Find Match pulses every free tile matching your current selection.
  • Shuffle randomizes the kinds on whatever tiles remain — useful if you get stuck.
  • Change Layout (top of the page) lets you pick a different board shape before starting a new game.
  • The zoom slider on the right edge scales the board up from its default fit — bigger tiles, more scrolling within the table itself.
