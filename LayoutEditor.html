How To Use

Layout Editor
  • Click an empty grid point to place a tile on the current Layer tab. Click an existing tile to select it.
  • Drag a selection to move it, or use the D-pad (or arrow keys) to nudge it one snap-step at a time.
  • Multi-select: click-drag an empty area to box-select several tiles at once, or Ctrl-click to add/remove individual tiles from the selection. A multi-tile selection moves as one rigid group; if that group started directly on top of tiles on a lower layer, you'll be asked whether to bring those along too.
  • Every tile-width has 2 snap points across and 2 down — the same half-tile offsets Turtle/Pyramid/Irish Castle use.
  • The ★ marks the grid origin — build outward from it so left/right symmetry stays easy to eyeball. Center shifts the whole layout so it's centered on the star.
  • Mirror has two independent switches, H (left/right) and V (top/bottom) — either auto-places/drags a mirrored copy of what you place or move; turn both on for 4-way symmetry (up to 4 tiles at once, equal distance from the star on every side). A given partner only comes along if it actually exists on the board — placing/moving a tile that has no H, V, or diagonal partner just affects that one tile.
  • Tiles can't be placed or dragged on top of another tile on the same layer — different layers overlapping is normal (that's how "covered from above" works), same-layer overlap isn't a valid board state.
  • Undo (or Ctrl/Cmd+Z) steps back through every change — placing, moving, removing, Center, Clear Grid, Load — up to 50 steps.
  • Test Solvability checks whether the current tiles (any layer) can be fully cleared in some order, using the same free-tile rule Mahjong.html plays by.
  • Export is blocked unless the tile count is even — an odd count can never be fully paired off. It does NOT require exactly 144, but Mahjong.html's current tile-kind generator specifically expects 144 to actually play a layout, so anything else will export fine but won't load into a game yet.
  • Load opens a previously exported .json (or any of Turtle/Pyramid/Irish Castle's own files) back into the grid, re-centered on the origin star. Reload re-applies the last file you loaded, discarding edits made since — different from Undo (one step at a time) and New (empties the grid).
