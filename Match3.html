How to Play

Match 3 - Gravity Match
  • Swap two adjacent tiles to line up 3 or more of the same kind in a row or column.
  • Matched tiles clear, everything above falls to fill the gap, and new tiles drop in from the top.
  • A run of exactly 4 creates a striped tile — clears its whole row or column.
  • A run of 5+ in a straight line creates a Color Bomb — swap it with any tile to clear every tile of that tile's color.
  • A T or L shaped match creates a Bomb — explodes the 3x3 area around it on its next swap or match, not the instant it's created.
  • A 2x2 block match creates a Fish — seeks out and clears a blocker tile if one exists, otherwise clears up to 5 tiles of whichever color it was swapped with.
  • Swap two specials directly into each other for a combo: two Bombs together set off two full 5x5 explosions back to back; a Bomb + Color Bomb clears every tile of the Bomb's color; a Bomb + Striped clears a 3-row-by-3-column plus sign; two Stripes of the same direction clear 3 rows/columns instead of 1, opposite directions clear a plus shape; a Color Bomb + Striped turns every tile of the Striped candy's color into a Striped candy and detonates them all at once; two Fish sweep up to 5 blockers (backfilled with random tiles if fewer exist); two Color Bombs clear the entire board.
  • Blockers never join a normal match — they're cleared when any match or special effect lands directly next to them (any side), or by a Fish seeking one out; clearing one never triggers a special effect of its own, and every blocker clears in a single hit. Every difficulty starts with some already on the field, more on harder tiers, and more keep falling in from the top over time as the game goes on (see Chain below). Easy/Medium's blockers are single cells that fall and drift with gravity like any other tile; Hard's starting batch is solid instead — several bigger, fixed, multi-cell wall chunks that never move, the kind you'd see blocking off part of the board in other match-3 games. Set difficulty in Settings — it applies in both Endless and Classic.
  • The longer a single game runs, the more often blockers fall in — a hidden chain counter nudges the odds up move by move, creeping toward (but never quite reaching) the next difficulty's rate. Starting a new game resets it.
  • Tap a special tile by itself to activate it in place, or swap/drag it into any neighbor — both work, matched or not.
  • Any powerup still sitting un-activated on the board when a game ends gets banked into your Powerup Wallet (🎒, top left) instead of wasted. Spend a banked one on any tile in a later game for free — no move spent — or let it ride: whatever's still banked when you win a Classic round cashes out as bonus score in an end-of-round cascade.
  • Hint picks the best move it can find, in order: one that clears a blocker, one that creates a new powerup, swapping an existing powerup directly, one that at least sets up a powerup once things settle — a plain match only if none of those exist. Shuffle rearranges the board (also happens automatically if no move is left) — neither costs a move.
  • In Classic mode (Settings > Mode), reach the target score to win — there's no move limit, so take your time. Endless mode has no target at all, just keep playing.
