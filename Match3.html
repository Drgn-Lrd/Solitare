How to Play

Match 3 - Gravity Match
  • Swap two adjacent tiles to line up 3 or more of the same kind in a row or column.
  • Matched tiles clear, everything above falls to fill the gap, and new tiles drop in from the top.
  • A run of exactly 4 creates a striped tile — matching or swapping it clears its whole row or column.
  • A run of 5 or more creates a bomb — swapping it with any tile clears every tile of that tile's color.
  • Swapping a special tile into any neighbor activates it immediately, even without a new match.
  • Hint highlights one swap you can currently make. Shuffle rearranges the board (also happens automatically if no move is left).
