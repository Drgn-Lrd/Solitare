How to Play

Match 3 - Gravity Match
  • Swap two adjacent tiles to line up 3 or more of the same kind in a row or column.
  • Matched tiles clear, everything above falls to fill the gap, and new tiles drop in from the top.
  • A run of exactly 4 creates a striped tile — clears its whole row or column.
  • A run of 5+ in a straight line creates a Color Bomb — swap it with any tile to clear every tile of that tile's color.
  • A T or L shaped match creates a Bomb — explodes the 3x3 area around it on its next swap or match, not the instant it's created.
  • A 2x2 block match creates a Fish — seeks out and clears a blocker tile if one exists, otherwise clears up to 5 tiles of whichever color it was swapped with.
  • Swap two specials directly into each other for a combo: two Bombs together explode a bigger 5x5 area; a Bomb + Color Bomb clears every tile of the Bomb's color; two Stripes of the same direction clear 3 rows/columns instead of 1, and opposite directions clear a 3-wide plus shape; two Fish sweep up to 5 blockers (backfilled with random tiles if fewer exist); two Color Bombs clear the entire board.
  • Blockers are immovable — they never join a normal match and only clear via a special's effect. Longer cascade chains have a chance to drop one onto the board.
  • Tap a special tile by itself to activate it in place, or swap/drag it into any neighbor — both work, matched or not.
  • Hint highlights one swap you can currently make. Shuffle rearranges the board (also happens automatically if no move is left) — neither costs a move.
  • In Classic mode (Settings > Mode), reach the target score before you run out of moves. Endless mode has no target or move limit.
