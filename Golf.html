How to Play

Golf Solitaire
  • Play the exposed (bottom) card of any tableau column onto the foundation pile if it's exactly one rank higher or lower — suit doesn't matter.
  • Ranks never wrap: a King can't take an Ace, and an Ace can't take a King — unless you turn on Wrap in Settings (Easy Golf variant).
  • Tap the stock pile to flip a new card onto the foundation when you're stuck. There's no reshuffle — once the stock runs out, that's it.
  • Clear all 7 columns to win. If you run out of moves with cards still on the table, the round ends there.
  • Undo reverses your last move. Hint highlights one move you can currently make. Auto Play plays any column that's currently the only one able to move.
  • Score follows real Golf scoring: strokes = stock draws + cards left on the table. Lower is better — zero is a perfect round.
