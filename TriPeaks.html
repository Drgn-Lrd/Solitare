How to Play

TriPeaks Solitaire
  • 28 cards form three peaks. Only the bottom row starts face-up — a card higher up flips face-up once BOTH cards directly beneath it are cleared.
  • Tap any uncovered peak card that's exactly one rank above or below the top waste card to play it.
  • By default Ace and King count as adjacent (K → A → 2) — turn this off in Settings for stricter rules.
  • Tap the stock pile to draw a new waste card when nothing's playable. By default the stock reshuffles from the waste once it runs out (Continuous Draw, toggle off in Settings for a single pass with no reshuffle).
  • Clear all 28 peak cards to win — cards left in the stock don't matter.
  • Thoughtful Mode (Settings, off by default) shows the real faces of still-covered cards, dimmed, so you can plan ahead — they're still unplayable until uncovered.
  • Undo reverses your last move. Hint highlights one move you can currently make.

Ace/King Wrap
When on, Ace and King count as one rank apart (K→A→2), same as most TriPeaks implementations. Turn off for stricter Ace-low-only rules.
Continuous Draw
When on, an empty stock reshuffles the waste pile back into it so you can keep drawing indefinitely. Turn off for a single pass through the stock with no reshuffle.
