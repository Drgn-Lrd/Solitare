How to Play

Bubble Shooter
  • Move your mouse (or drag a finger) over the board to aim.
  • Release to fire the glowing bubble at the pivot toward the aim line.
  • Match 3 or more of the same element to pop them.
  • Bubbles left floating with nothing connecting them to the top fall away too, for a bonus.
  • Every few shots a new row pushes down from the ceiling — don't let bubbles reach the dashed danger line.
  • Undo reverses your last shot. Hint highlights a cluster your current bubble can complete.
