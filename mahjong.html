How to Play

Mahjong Solitaire
  • Match pairs of identical tiles to remove them from the board.
  • A tile is only playable if it is "free"—meaning no other tile is directly on top of it, and it has no tile touching its left OR right side.
  • Flower and Season tiles are exceptions: any Flower matches any other Flower, and any Season matches any other Season.
  • Clear all 144 tiles to win!
